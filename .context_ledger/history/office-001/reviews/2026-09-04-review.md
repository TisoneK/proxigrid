## What was built

**Persistence (`src/lib/research/store/research-store.ts`)**
- `PrismaValidationLedger` — the §9 guard made durable: `Experiment(kind="oos")` rows carry the specHash in their `window` JSON; the ledger consults those rows, so validation windows stay consumed across restarts/redeploys (the in-memory ledger reset every boot).
- `ResearchStore` — PXG-### code allocation (max+1), strategy creation, experiment recording (specHash injected into `window`; Json-typed fields normalized for Prisma), and `transitionStrategy` applying the pure state machine + evidentiary guards (→ PAPER needs a passing OOS experiment; a PXG-000 sentinel holds ledger-only records).

**API**
- `POST /api/research/run` — fetches live candles (default 1000, clamp 100–1000), builds grid candidates (MA/RSI named-strategy sweeps + feature-threshold hypotheses over the builtin registry), runs the lab, persists every strategy + experiment, returns records/outcome stages/survivors. `GET /api/research/strategies[?status=]`, `GET /api/research/strategies/{id}` (detail + legal transitions), `POST /api/research/strategies/{id}` (guarded transition; illegal → 409).

**UI (`research-lab.tsx` + `use-research.ts`)**
- Research Lab card on the dashboard: "Run lab" one-click, strategy roster with lifecycle color badges and best-OOS return, expandable rows with experiment metrics (trades/win-rate/Sharpe/max-DD), critic-check chips, and the legal next transitions as buttons (guard rejections surface as error toasts). Empty state explains the loop.
